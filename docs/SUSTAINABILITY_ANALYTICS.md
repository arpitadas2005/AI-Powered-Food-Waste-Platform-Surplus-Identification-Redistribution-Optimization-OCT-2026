## Milestone 3 — Phase 3 Deliverable

## 1. Executive Summary

The **Sustainability Impact Analytics Engine** quantifies the verified environmental, social, and financial benefits generated by diverting surplus edible food from landfills into charitable redistribution channels.

All calculations are grounded in completed physical donation records (`surplus_claims` where `status == COMPLETED`) and comply with internationally accepted scientific and governmental standards:
* **EPA Waste Reduction Model (WARM v15)** for greenhouse gas emissions reduction.
* **USDA / Feeding America Standards** for nutritional meal equivalents.
* **EPA Greenhouse Gas Equivalencies** for real-world environmental comparisons.
* **National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST)** guidance for spreadsheet formula injection prevention (CWE-1236).

---

## 2. Standardized Environmental & Social Conversion Factors

### 2.1 Avoided Greenhouse Gas (GHG) Emissions
When food decomposes in municipal solid waste landfills under anaerobic conditions, it generates methane ($CH_4$), a greenhouse gas with a global warming potential 28 to 36 times greater than carbon dioxide ($CO_2$) over a 100-year timescale. Furthermore, food waste diversion saves the upstream life-cycle emissions embedded in agriculture, processing, packaging, and cold-chain transport.

$$\text{Avoided } CO_2e \text{ (kg)} = \text{Rescued Mass (kg)} \times 2.50\text{ kg } CO_2e/\text{kg}$$

* **Source**: United States Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) Waste Reduction Model (WARM) v15, Food Waste Category (Commercial/Institutional Diversion) & FAO Food Wastage Footprint methodology.

### 2.2 Meal Equivalents Provided
To convert physical food mass into standardized social meal metrics for hunger relief reporting, the platform applies the consensus benchmark defined by Feeding America and the USDA:

$$\text{Meal Equivalents} = \left\lfloor \frac{\text{Rescued Mass (kg)}}{0.42\text{ kg/meal}} \right\rfloor \approx \text{Rescued Mass (kg)} \times 2.381$$

* **Source**: USDA Economic Research Service & Feeding America standard meal definition (1.2 pounds $= 0.42$ kilograms per meal equivalent).

### 2.3 Tangible Environmental Equivalencies
To translate abstract carbon metrics into relatable consumer equivalents:
1. **Urban Tree Seedlings Grown for 10 Years**:
   $$\text{Tree Equivalents} = \frac{\text{Avoided } CO_2e \text{ (kg)}}{21.77\text{ kg } CO_2/\text{tree}}$$
   *(EPA standard: Average urban tree seedling sequestering carbon over a 10-year growth period).*
2. **Passenger Vehicle Miles Avoided**:
   $$\text{Car Miles Avoided} = \frac{\text{Avoided } CO_2e \text{ (kg)}}{0.393\text{ kg } CO_2e/\text{mile}}$$
   *(EPA standard: Average passenger vehicle emitting 393 grams $CO_2e$ per mile driven).*

### 2.4 Financial Loss Prevention (Retail Value Rescued)
Calculated using the lot-level purchase or production cost basis recorded in the inventory ledger:

$$\text{Total Rescued Value (\$)} = \sum_{c \in \text{CompletedClaims}} (c.\text{claimed\_quantity} \times c.\text{listing}.\text{item}.\text{cost\_per\_unit})$$

## 3. Strict Status Integrity Gate

To guarantee auditability and eliminate fabricated metrics:
* **Included**: Claims in `COMPLETED` status where physical handover and receipt have been verified.
* **Excluded**: Claims in `REQUESTED`, `PENDING`, `ACCEPTED`, `APPROVED`, `READY_FOR_PICKUP`, `REJECTED`, `CANCELLED`, or `EXPIRED` status.

---

## 4. Category-Wise Distribution

Metrics are aggregated at the category taxonomy level (`categories` table) to identify which sectors yield the greatest diversion impact:
* **Dairy & Eggs**: High embedded carbon intensity; critical cold-chain monitoring.
* **Produce**: High mass volume and moisture content (primary landfill methane generator).
* **Bakery**: High calorie density and rapid turnaround.
* **Prepared Meals**: Immediate consumption value.
* **Meat & Poultry**: Highest upstream life-cycle emissions avoided per kilogram.

## 5. Security: Spreadsheet Formula Injection Sanitization (CWE-1236)

CSV export endpoints are susceptible to Formula Injection if user-provided text (such as listing titles, addresses, or donor names) begins with executable formula operators (`=`, `+`, `-`, `@`, `\t`, `\r`). When opened in Microsoft Excel or Google Sheets, such cells could execute arbitrary code or exfiltrate data.

The platform neutralizes this vulnerability across all CSV export columns via `sanitize_csv_cell`:
```python
def sanitize_csv_cell(value: Any) -> str:
    if value is None:
        return ""
    text = str(value)
    if text and text[0] in ("=", "+", "-", "@", "\t", "\r"):
        return f"'{text}"
    return text
```
Any string beginning with a dangerous operator is prefixed with an apostrophe (`'`), forcing spreadsheet software to treat the cell strictly as literal plain text.
